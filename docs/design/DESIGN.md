# truelift design system

truelift's theme, adapted in G15 (2026-11-24) from the house design system for ADK agent interfaces. It keeps the house structure, components, layout, motion and accessibility floor, and changes the look: bright monotone surfaces with white cards and a light sidebar, monotone chrome with deep green for actions and links, pale gold icon chips with two-tone icons (deep green stroke over a gold fill), gold for the brand mark, icon fills and charts (money colours only), a larger fluid type scale with Schibsted Grotesk for headings and figures and Manrope (weight 450) for the interface, softer and rounder surfaces (12 px cards, 16 px feature cards), one soft card shadow in light mode, and a landing page that says what the product does. Style references: coder.com, flick.social and jasper.ai (their CSS: display grotesks, fluid headings, body at weight 450, 12–24 px radii); their content and brands are not used. Map it to the shadcn theme file (`frontend/src/app/theme.css`) as described under Colors and keep the two in step. The format is Google's DESIGN.md (`version: alpha`); lint it with `npx -y @google/design.md@0.4.0 lint docs/design/DESIGN.md` and treat any error **or warning** in the JSON `summary` as a failure, because 0.4.0 reports contrast failures as warnings and exits 0 on them.

## Overview

A clear, confident working tool for marketing leaders who read, check and decide on what the analysis found. It should feel bright, precise and current: a near-neutral light canvas with white cards that sit slightly above it, a light sidebar set apart by a hairline, monotone chrome (greys for fills, selection and the theme switch), deep green only for the main action, links, the current step and icon strokes, gold only for the brand mark, icon chips and charts, and a firm grotesk for headings and figures. Colour is limited to money colours: green and gold, plus red for errors only. Depth comes from tonal layers, one quiet card shadow and figures shown as figures, never from decoration. It does not look "AI": no gradients, glass, glow, neon or purple, no sparkle icons and no animated backgrounds (house rules, kept on purpose in G15; the faint hero grid is the one pattern).

- **Density:** comfortable by default (36 px controls, 44 px landing calls to action, 36 px table rows); compact mode (32 px controls and rows) for data-dense screens only.
- **Copy:** sentence case everywhere — buttons, titles, menu items, column headers, tabs. Plain verbs on buttons ("Approve", "Retry"). No exclamation marks and no first-person claims of feeling.
- **Navigation state:** a selected item keeps its size and turns semibold (600) on its selected fill, with a 2 px `sidebar-primary` marker on its left in the sidebar; it never switches to a smaller type token. Labels reserve their bold width so neighbours do not move.
- **Accessibility floor:** WCAG 2.2 AA. Every text pair, at any size, is at least 4.5:1. Field edges, focus indicators, checkbox and radio edges, chart marks and the check-before-use underline are at least 3:1 against what they sit on. Pointer targets are at least 24 × 24 px; run axe with the `target-size` rule switched on, because axe-core 4.14.0 ships it disabled.
- **Two sections follow the eight spec sections:** Motion and Brand override. The linter keeps them as unknown headings.

## Colors

Colour token names are the shadcn CSS variable names; tokens prefixed `dark-` are the same variables under `.dark`. Map them one to one into the theme file named by the `tailwind.css` field of `components.json`: `colors.muted-foreground` becomes `--muted-foreground` in `:root`, and `colors.dark-muted-foreground` becomes `--muted-foreground` in `.dark`. Keep the `oklch()` values exactly as written. `primary-hover`, `primary-active`, `destructive-foreground`, `success`, `warning`, `info`, the four `*-subtle` surfaces and `sidebar-muted-foreground` are additions: declare them in both scopes and register each in `@theme inline` as `--color-<name>: var(--<name>)`. Never use raw Tailwind palette colours (`text-green-600`) or `dark:` colour overrides; use the tokens.

- **Neutrals and layers:** near-neutral (chroma at most 0.012, a hint cool at hue 250–260). The canvas `background` (#F6F7F8) sits a clear step below `card` and `popover` (#FEFEFF), so cards read as raised white surfaces; `muted` (#F0F1F3) and `secondary` are the bands and quiet fills inside cards (stat tiles, code, table headers). `foreground` is near-black (#111419), never pure black.
- **Sidebar (light):** `sidebar` (#F9FAFB in light, #08090C in dark) with a `sidebar-border` hairline. Items in `sidebar-foreground`, captions in `sidebar-muted-foreground`; hover and the selected item on `sidebar-accent` (= `selected`) in foreground text, the selected item semibold with a 2 px `sidebar-primary` marker, a brighter green than `primary-ink` (#087C46 light, so it does not read as black; #73CD9F dark). The brand mark is a gold rising arrow on a near-black chip.
- **Deep green (main) and gold (accent):** `primary` (#0C5C3C, hover #074C31, active #033D26) is the main action, the active step circle and selected marks, with white `primary-foreground` (7.9:1). `primary-ink` is the same deep green for text and marks on light surfaces: links, eyebrows, check icons, the hero's key words, the selected sidebar marker, the chosen option's border, step joints and `ring` (focus). - **Neutral fills (monotone chrome):** `accent` (#EBEDEF) for hover rows, the theme switch and a pressed toggle, `selected` (#EFF0F2 light, #1F2123 dark) for the selected sidebar item, both with foreground text. - **Icon chips (component `IconChip`):** a square chip (6 px radius; 48 px with a 24 px icon on landing and project cards, 40 px with a 20 px icon in card headers) in `chip` (#FCEABE pale gold light, #3F3017 gold-brown dark) with a two-tone icon: the stroke in `chip-foreground` (#0C5C3C, 6.8:1 light; #F6E7C3, 10.4:1 dark) at 1.75 px over closed shapes filled with `chip-fill` (gold; the stroke clears 5.1:1 light and 3.5:1 dark over it). Use only icons built from closed shapes: an open axis or zigzag path fills into a stray triangle. The brand mark stays separate: a gold arrow on near-black `brand`.
- **Outlined landing cards:** feature cards and project cards use a 1 px `card-edge` border (a soft deep green in light mode, a lifted grey-green in dark mode) instead of the hairline, so they stand out from the grey canvas; the resting shadow stays.
- **Semantic colours (money colours only):** `success` (#217937, a brighter green than the deep main green) carries positive status; `warning` uses the deep gold (#845A0F) with its icon and word, so it never relies on colour; `info` is neutral grey (#51565C); `destructive` (#B2403D) stays red for errors only, because an error must be unmistakable. Chroma 0.15 or less (house rule). Status is always also said in words or an icon. Charts lead with green (`chart-1` #1A8656 light, #64C591 dark, used by every single-series bar and progress bar), then gold (`chart-2` #A97416), deep gold, grey and deep green.
- **Status surfaces (tinted, subtle):** `success-subtle` (#E1F7E3), `warning-subtle` (#FDF1D8, a pale gold), `destructive-subtle` (#FEEBE9) and `info-subtle` (#ECEFF1, neutral) sit at L about 0.955; in dark mode L 0.27 (#172C1C, #2E2511, #3A1D1B, #252729). Each carries its own semantic colour as text, plus `foreground` and `muted-foreground`. They are fills, never borders, and never a whole region or page.
- **Lines:** `border` (#E1E3E5) is a decorative hairline between regions and may be below 3:1. `input` (#83868B) is the edge of a field, checkbox or control that has no other boundary, and must stay at 3:1 or more.
- **Dark mode is designed, not inverted:** surfaces step up in lightness (background #0D0E11, card #16181C, popover #1D1F23, muted #1E2124) so layers read without shadows; the sidebar steps below the canvas (#08090C); edges are opaque (`dark-input` #75797F), never white at 10–15%.

- **Two sans roles:** Schibsted Grotesk (a firm grotesk in the Helvetica and Forma family) sets the landing headline (`hero`), page titles (`display`), section titles (`headline-lg`, `headline-md`) and big figures (`metric`); Manrope sets everything else, card titles and leads included. Commercial faces (Manifold CF, Forma DJR, Helvetica) cannot ship in this public repository.
- **Scale (fluid display, stepped text):** display sizes grow with the viewport, so the tokens above give their desktop maximum: `hero` `clamp(2.5rem, 5vw, 4rem)` / 1.05, `display` `clamp(1.875rem, 3vw, 2.5rem)` / 1.15, `headline-lg` `clamp(1.5rem, 2.4vw, 2rem)` / 1.2, `headline-md` `clamp(1.25rem, 1.8vw, 1.5rem)` / 1.3, `metric` `clamp(1.75rem, 2.5vw, 2.25rem)` / 1.1. - **Implementation:** turn each token into an `@utility type-<token>` in the theme file. Do not register `--text-*` keys for them, because shadcn's `cn()` treats unknown `text-*` classes as colours. `--font-heading` stays on the sans (component titles). Declare `--font-*` in a plain `@theme` block (not `inline`) so a brand can override them at run time.

## Layout

- **Grid:** an 8-point rhythm with 4 px half-steps. Tailwind's `--spacing` stays `0.25rem`; use the steps 4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 48 and 64 px (`spacing` tokens). Other values need a reason.
- **Density:** comfortable — controls 36 px (`h-9`), landing calls to action 44 px (`h-11`, button size `lg`), table rows 36 px, card padding 16 px (20 px from 768 px), 20 px between card sections. Compact — controls and rows 32 px, card padding 12 px, for data-dense screens only, switched per screen and never mixed in one view. No target below 24 px.
- **Page:** every screen centres its own column: the landing page at most 1152 px (`max-w-6xl`), app screens at most 1024 px (`max-w-5xl`), so the page header, stepper and cards line up and a wide window never leaves an empty band on one side. Narrow things cap themselves inside cards (fields `max-w-md`, prose about 68ch). Side gutter 16 px below 768 px, 24 px from 768 px, 32 px from 1280 px. No horizontal page scroll at 375 px; wide tables and code scroll inside their own container.
- **App shell:** a sidebar, then a 48 px header and the work area. The sidebar is expanded (256 px) from 1024 px, an icon rail (72 px: icons only, labels kept for screen readers and as `title` tooltips) from 768 px, so a tablet keeps its room for the work area, and a top bar below 768 px. The sidebar starts with Home (selected only on the landing page), then AI provider and the projects. A project's sidebar link points at the project, so it stays selected on every step. The header keeps one line at every width: sidebar toggle, conversation title (truncated), environment badge, side-panel toggle, and a new-conversation icon below 768 px.
- **Conversation column:** one centred column at most 768 px wide; answer prose at about 68ch; 32 px between turns. The thread scrolls inside its own region; the composer floats over its bottom edge with a 24 px fade above it, and the thread has bottom padding and `scroll-padding-bottom` at least the composer's height so the last line and focused items are never hidden behind it.
- **Side panel:** at 1024 px and wider, a shadcn `Resizable` panel beside the conversation, 400 px by default, 360–480 px, inset 8 px from the edges. Below 1024 px the same content opens in a right-hand `Sheet`. Its open state lives in the URL (`?panel=sources`).
- **Empty state:** its own screen, never beside a thread. The greeting sits at about 38% of the viewport height, the composer directly under it, the suggestions under that, in a column at most 720 px wide.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth comes from surface steps (light sidebar, cool canvas, white cards), hairlines and two quiet shadows.

- **Resting elevation, cards only, light mode only:** cards and feature cards use a soft, wide shadow, `0 1px 2px rgb(15 23 42 / 0.04), 0 8px 24px -12px rgb(15 23 42 / 0.12)` (`--card-shadow`, utility `shadow-card`), plus their hairline, so they sit just above the canvas. Dark mode has none on purpose: a shadow does not show on a near-black canvas, so a dark card is a step lighter (#16181C on #0D0E11) instead. The landing feature cards deepen it on hover (`--card-shadow-hover`, utility `shadow-card-hover`, with the 4 px lift under Motion). Panels, tables, inputs and messages stay flat (`--shadow-xs: 0 0 #0000`).
- **Floating elevation, floating layers only:** popovers, menus, tooltips, hover cards, dialogs, sheets and the composer use `0 1px 2px rgb(15 23 42 / 0.06), 0 8px 24px rgb(15 23 42 / 0.08)` plus their hairline. The composer is the only in-flow element with it, because it floats over the thread. Nothing else casts a shadow.
- **Scroll fade:** the thread fades into the canvas over 24 px above the composer (`linear-gradient` from transparent to `background`). It shows that text continues underneath; it is not a decorative gradient, and no other gradient is allowed.
- **Hero grid (the one pattern):** behind the landing hero only, 1 px lines in the `border` colour at half strength every 40 px (utility `bg-grid`), at full strength down to the middle of the headline (160 px, a fixed length so it lands on the headline at every width), then a slow fade over the rest of the hero: 50% at its halfway point, 30% at 70%, 15% at 88%, and fully blended into the canvas at the section's bottom edge,  so text over it stays easy to read. It is drawn with hard-stop lines, not a colour gradient; no other pattern, glow or texture is allowed.
- **Dark mode:** separate layers by surface lightness (`dark-popover` is lighter than `dark-card`; the composer uses `popover`); keep the hairline, drop every shadow by setting `--shadow-md`, `--shadow-lg` and `--card-shadow` to `0 0 #0000` under `.dark`.
- **Scrims:** dialogs and sheets dim the page with the light-mode `foreground` value (`oklch(0.21 0.02 262 / 0.4)`) in both modes, so the scrim darkens in dark mode too; no blur.

## Shapes

- **Radius steps:** 6 px (`rounded.sm`: menu items, inline code, citation chips, checkboxes, progress bars), 8 px (`rounded.md`: buttons, inputs, selects, tabs, sidebar rows, code blocks, icon chips), 12 px (`rounded.lg`: cards, notices, stat tiles, the approval card, popovers, message bubbles) and 16 px (`rounded.xl`: feature cards, the landing example card, the composer, the side panel, sheets and dialogs). `full` is for badges, the announcement pill, step circles, avatars and status dots.
- **Tailwind mapping:** pin the steps instead of deriving them: `--radius-sm` and `--radius-md` 6px, `--radius-lg` 8px (`rounded-lg`: controls), `--radius-xl` 12px (`rounded-xl`: cards), `--radius-2xl`, `--radius-3xl` and `--radius-4xl` 16px (`rounded-2xl`), so stock classes cannot become larger than 16 px. Badges use `rounded-full`. Declare the radius steps in a plain `@theme` block so a brand's control radius can override `--radius-lg` at run time.
- **Lines:** 1 px hairlines. Focus is a 2 px solid `ring` outline with a 2 px offset, drawn with `outline` (it survives forced-colours mode), replacing shadcn's `ring-3 ring-ring/50` box-shadow. Implement it as an unlayered `:focus-visible` rule that also sets `--tw-ring-shadow: 0 0 #0000`, and remove `outline-ring/50` from the base layer. The composer draws the outline on its whole card while its text area has focus (`:has(textarea:focus-visible)`), not inside the field.
- **Icons:** Lucide, 16 px in controls and 14 px in chips and source rows, 1.5–2 px stroke, `currentColor`. No filled or multicolour icons, and few icons in navigation: conversation rows carry none.

## Components

Use shadcn/ui (Base UI) components first; edit their `cva` variants to these rules rather than overriding with `className`. Control heights move together across button, input, select, toggle and tabs.

- **Buttons:** primary for the single main action in a region; secondary or outline for the rest; ghost for icon buttons in toolbars and message footers (with a tooltip and an `aria-label`); destructive only when the effect destroys something. Labels in `label-md` (600). Hover darkens to `primary-hover` in light mode and lightens slightly in dark mode, press goes to `primary-active`; no translate, scale or opacity-dimmed hover. The label on a primary fill (buttons, the active step) uses `type-label-primary`: `label-md` at weight 600 in light mode and 700 in dark mode, because dark text on the light green fill reads thinner. Every enabled button, select, radio, file button and `label[for]` shows a pointer cursor; disabled controls show `not-allowed`.
- **Inputs and selects:** `card` fill, `input` edge, 8 px radius, 36 px tall; the error state adds a `destructive` edge and a message in words below the field.
- **Badges:** pill shape (`rounded-full`), 24 px tall, `caption` type, `secondary` fill or outline; status badges use the matching `*-subtle` fill with the semantic colour as text and add an icon or word, never colour alone.
- **Cards and panels:** hairline border, 12 px radius, the light-mode resting shadow. A card header may lead with an icon chip (40 px `IconChip`, pale gold with a 20 px two-tone icon). In the conversation thread, the approval card is the only bordered card.
- **Sticky action bar:** when a card holds the one action a step needs (Allow sharing, Confirm roles), its footer sticks to the bottom of the viewport while the card is taller than the screen: solid `card` surface, a hairline above, the card's bottom radius. Never auto-scroll the page to an action; it moves the content under the reader and breaks focus.
- **Callout (extra information):** supporting explanation under a primary card (for example "Why truelift needs an AI model" under the key card) is a callout, not a section with its own header: a `muted` box (12 px radius, 16–20 px padding) with an info icon in `primary-ink`, the title inline in semibold followed by one muted sentence, and any items as plain lines, each led by a check in `primary-ink`. No chips or cards inside a callout. Keep it short: what the landing page already explains is not repeated.
- **Page header:** every screen starts with one: an eyebrow (`caption`, uppercase with wide tracking, `primary-ink`; the one all-caps label, one or two words), the title in `display`, and one `lead` line in `muted-foreground`.
- **Stepper:** the steps of a project as numbered 32 px circles joined by hairlines (the joint turns `primary` once the step after it is done). The active step is a filled `primary` circle with a semibold label; a done step shows a check on `accent`; each step has a `caption` status under its label ("Uploaded", "To review", "Allowed", "2 to fix", "Confirmed") in `success` when done. Status comes only from what the API or the current session knows; never show a tick that might be false. Below 768 px the stepper becomes a vertical accordion instead of scrolling sideways: one header box per step (card, hairline, 12 px radius, the same circle, label and caption, a chevron), the current step's header with a `primary-ink` border and its screen directly below it; tapping another header moves to that step and closes the current one, and tapping the open header folds its screen away (`aria-expanded`). It is a labelled section, not a nav, because it holds the screen.
- **Landing page:** a centred hero on the grid pattern (announcement pill, `hero` headline with the key words in `primary-ink` (deep green text, no highlighter or fill), a `lead`, a primary and an outline call to action at 44 px, and a row of checks for the privacy facts), then an example result card clearly labelled "Example · illustrative numbers", as wide as the sections below it (`max-w-6xl`) with its two halves 48–64 px apart. Then "How it works" (four numbered feature cards) on a full-bleed `section` band (a barely darker step of the canvas, #F1F3F5 on #F6F7F8 light and #090B0D on #0D0E11 dark, so the change is felt more than seen, with hairlines above and below and 64–80 px padding), then "What you get" (three feature cards) on the canvas, each under a centred section title, then a hairline (`hr`, `border`) and the projects, then the closing call to action and the footer. No invented logos, testimonials or statistics.
- **Closing call to action (dark band):** the last section of the landing page, after the projects: a 16 px-radius `band` box (the near-black brand colour in light mode, a deep green-black in dark mode) inside the page column with a `gold` eyebrow, a `headline-lg` title and a `lead` in `band-muted`, then one gold button (`button` variant `gold`: `gold` fill, `brand` label, `gold-hover` on hover) and a plain underlined link in `band-foreground`. The focus ring inside it is `gold`, because the green ring would not show on the band. The only dark region on the page.
- **Footer:** one line of `caption` text in `muted-foreground` under the band, 32 px below it: "© 2026 the truelift authors · MIT licence" at the start and "Runs on this computer · your data stays local" at the end (stacked below 640 px). No links, logos or columns.
- **Tabs:** the `line` variant in panels; inactive tabs in full `muted-foreground`, the active tab in `foreground` with the 2 px underline.

App shell and screens around the conversation:

- **Sidebar:** the light `sidebar` surface, with a hairline to the work area and no borders between items; at 768 px and wider it stays in view while the work area scrolls, and below 768 px it is a top bar: on scroll the brand row and theme switch move away while the nav links stay pinned at the top (the bar is sticky with a negative offset equal to the brand row, 52 px). The brand mark is a 32 px near-black `brand` square (with a `sidebar-border` ring so it holds its edge on the dark sidebar) with a `gold` Lucide `TrendingUp` icon (the lift) beside the product name in the display font; the same mark is the favicon (`public/favicon.svg`). Header: the workspace or client logo slot (logo image, workspace name in `label-md`, product name in `caption`), then "New conversation" and "About this assistant". Content: conversation history grouped by date ("Today", "Yesterday", "Previous 7 days", "Previous 30 days"), group labels in `caption` and `sidebar-muted-foreground`, 32 px rows with titles truncated, the current item marked with `sidebar-accent`, the left marker and `aria-current="page"`. Footer: the user (initials avatar, name, role) opening a menu with theme and sign out. In icon mode the history hides and the remaining items show tooltips.
- **Header:** conversation title (`body-md`, 500, truncated) as the page `h1`, the environment badge, then the side-panel toggle (`aria-pressed`). Nothing else.
- **Environment badge:** an `info-subtle` badge naming the environment ("Pilot"; in replay mode, the replay label that demo readiness defines), with a visually hidden "Environment:" prefix. Never "Demo" or "Test".
- **Empty state:** a greeting in `display` (`headline-lg` below 640 px), one line on what the assistant can and cannot do ("I can read delivery and contract records and draft supplier emails. I cannot change contracts, and I ask before sending anything."), the composer, then a 2 × 2 grid of suggestion cards (one column below 640 px). Each card is a button on `card` with a hairline and 8 px radius: an icon in `muted-foreground`, the exact prompt it sends in `body-md` 500, and one muted line on the data it uses.

Agent conversation patterns (build on shadcn's AI Elements patterns and `Trace`; hand-build only where shadcn has no component):

- **Messages:** assistant answers are full-width prose in the column, no bubble, set in `answer-md`; user messages are compact `user-message` bubbles aligned to the end, `body-md`, at most 80% of the column. New turns enter with the turn entrance under Motion.
- **Per-answer actions:** a quiet footer of ghost icon buttons after each answer: Copy (keeps formatting and tables), Copy with sources (appends the numbered source list), Good response and Bad response (toggles with `aria-pressed`) and Retry.
- **Follow-ups:** after a finished answer, two or three follow-up prompts as small outline buttons (6 px radius) whose label is the exact prompt sent. None after a failed or running turn.
- **AI-generated label:** every assistant answer carries the words "AI-generated" in `ai-label` style (caption, `muted-foreground`, no icon glow or gradient). It opens a popover with the model, the sources used and the time of the answer. It is never decoration, never an action and never disabled; once a person edits the content, replace it with an "Edited" note and a revert control.
- **Streaming:** one subtle indicator at a time — shadcn's `shimmer` on the live status line ("Writing answer…"), or a 6 px `muted-foreground` caret at the end of the text. No spinners in the prose, no custom keyframes. Stop is reachable for the whole run. Announce completion, not each token: the transcript is an `aria-live="polite"` log, `aria-busy="true"` while streaming.
- **Tool steps while running:** one quiet `Trace` per run of steps, one `TraceLine` per step, `trace-line` style in `muted-foreground`. The title states the status in words and tense — "Running query…", "Ran query", "Failed to run query" — with an icon as a second cue; failures use `destructive` text plus the word "Failed". Running and finished steps stay visible while the turn is working. Rows become collapsible only once they have output; never dim a line with opacity. shadcn 4.22.1's `TraceDescription` ships `text-muted-foreground/70`, which axe measured at 3.3:1 on the house background (2.7:1 on stock tokens); change it to `text-muted-foreground`.
- **Folded steps:** once a turn finishes, its Trace folds into one summary line above the answer: duration, step count, failures in `destructive`, then scope or effects ("Worked for 4 s · 3 steps · 1 failed · searched 42 contracts", "Worked for 6 s · 2 steps · sent 3 emails after approval by Priya Shah at 14:02"), in `body-sm` `muted-foreground` with tabular figures and a chevron. It expands to the TraceLines. The separators are hidden from screen readers and replaced by commas.
- **No reasoning display:** do not show model reasoning text or a "Thought for 4 s" line. Show only real tool steps; reasoning text reads as proof and can be a rationalisation after the fact.
- **Citations:** a claim that rests on a source carries a numbered chip right after that claim, not at the end of the paragraph: `citation-chip` (caption, `muted` fill, `muted-foreground`, 4 px radius, 18 px tall), turning `accent` on hover. It is a real link with an accessible name that describes the source and place ("Source 1: Q3 delivery performance report, page 4"). Hover or focus opens a `HoverCard` (a `Popover` on touch) that quotes the supporting passage, says where it sits (page, clause, paragraph), gives host and date, and links "Open at passage". Do not put a row or block of source chips after the answer; end with a "View all 4 sources" ghost button that opens the side panel. Keep sources deduplicated by URL.
- **Check before use:** a figure, date or amount the model derived (calculated, converted or summed) rather than quoted gets a dotted 2 px `warning` underline, a tooltip that starts "Check before use." and says how it was derived, and visually hidden text with the same words. Quoted figures carry a citation instead. The line under the composer says "Check figures against the source before acting."
- **Side panel:** a `side-panel` (card surface, hairline, 12 px radius) with `line` tabs for Sources and Drafts and a Close button. Each source lists its number, title, document-type icon, host and date, what it was cited for, and the cited passage with its location, separated by hairlines rather than boxes. Drafts list each item waiting for approval with its status badge.
- **Approval card:** ask for approval only when an action has an effect; never for reads, and approve several similar effects as one batch. A pending approval is the one bordered `approval-card` in the thread, outside the Trace. Header: a Lucide icon for the action, the title ("Send 3 emails") in `title-md` and a "Waiting for approval" badge on `warning-subtle`. Body: a definition list with `label-md` `muted-foreground` labels — Action, Affects ("Brackwater Freight, Harbour Metals and Lindqvist Packaging (3 external recipients)"), Consequence ("Recipients see the emails immediately. Sent email cannot be recalled.") — then the exact item being approved (recipients, subject and full email text, or the ticket fields) on a `muted` band, in a collapsible that is open by default. Footer, aligned to the end: "Deny" (outline, never destructive) and "Approve" (primary; destructive variant only when the effect is destructive). It is a labelled `role="group"`, keyboard-operable in tab order with visible focus. When it appears while the reader is at the live edge, focus moves to it without trapping; when the reader has scrolled up, the view stays put, the jump-to-latest control shows a badge and the status region announces it. Once answered it collapses back to a Trace line ("Approved: sent 3 emails" or "Denied: send 3 emails") and the folded summary names the effect.
- **Error after a partial answer:** keep the text already shown. Below it, an unbordered notice on `destructive-subtle` (8 px radius) with an icon and the words in `destructive` — "Answer incomplete: the connection to the agent was lost. The text above is partial." — and "Retry" (outline, so it stands out from the tinted surface) that regenerates the turn. Never erase the partial text or replace it with a generic error.
- **Composer:** a two-row `composer` card: a text area at least 56 px tall above, a tool row below with ghost icon buttons on the left (Attach, and a sources toggle that names the sources in use, "Contract library, Delivery records", shown pressed on `accent`) and a 32 px primary icon button on the right — Send, replaced by Stop (a filled square) while a run is going. `popover` surface, `input` edge, 12 px radius, the floating shadow, no hairline across the column above it. The text area has a visible or visually hidden label. Enter sends, Shift+Enter adds a line, and Enter during IME composition does not send.
- **Data line:** one caption line under the composer, centred, in `muted-foreground`: where data is kept and how it is used, then what to check ("Data stays in the UK (europe-west2) and is not used for training. Check figures against the source before acting."). Use the deployment's real region and retention; in a demo, the fictional values must still be plausible for the stated region.

## Do's and Don'ts

- Do keep one primary action per region; don't put two primary buttons side by side.
- Do say status in words ("Ran", "Failed", "Incomplete"); don't rely on colour, opacity or an icon alone.
- Do keep the partial answer and offer Retry after a stream error; don't erase visible text or show only a toast.
- Do put the action, what it affects, the consequence and the exact item in the approval card before its buttons; don't ask "Are you sure?" without saying what happens, and don't hide the email text behind a closed preview.
- Do ask for approval only for actions with effects; don't gate reads or ask once per item in a batch.
- Do style Deny as outline; don't style Deny or Cancel as destructive.
- Do fold finished steps into one summary line and keep tool output collapsed; don't auto-expand them, reopen them after the person closed them, or show model reasoning text.
- Do use one subtle streaming indicator; don't stack spinners, typing dots and shimmer.
- Do put a numbered chip next to each claim, with the quoted passage in its preview; don't add a row of source chips after the answer or hide sources in a tooltip only.
- Do mark derived figures "check before use"; don't present a calculated amount as if it were quoted.
- Do answer in the first sentence; don't open with a greeting, praise of the question or a restatement.
- Do label assistant output "AI-generated" in words; don't use sparkles, glows, gradients or purple to mean AI.
- Do set answers at 17/28 with a 68ch measure; don't set long answers in 14 px across the full width.
- Do keep the approval card as the only bordered card in the thread; don't box sources, notices or steps in hairlines.
- Do use the `*-subtle` surfaces for status badges and notices; don't tint whole regions or use them as borders.
- Do keep the radius steps: 6 px chips, 8 px controls, 12 px cards, panels, the composer, sheets and dialogs, 16 px landing feature cards; don't round controls or chips to 12 px.
- Do give the composer the floating shadow and the canvas fade, and cards only the light-mode resting shadow; don't add other gradients (the hero grid mask is the one exception) or heavier shadows on static cards.
- Do show the empty state as its own screen; don't show it beside a running thread.
- Do use tokens through shadcn variables; don't use raw Tailwind palette colours, `dark:` colour overrides or hex in components.
- Do use a 2 px solid focus outline in `ring`; don't keep shadcn's `ring-ring/50` or remove outlines.
- Do keep secondary text at full `muted-foreground`; don't dim text with opacity modifiers such as `/70`.
- Do self-host fonts from `@fontsource-variable`; don't link a font CDN.
- Do set the theme class before first paint with a same-origin script (`public/theme-init.js`, a file because the CSP has no `'unsafe-inline'`) and `<meta name="color-scheme" content="light dark">`; don't rely on shadcn's Vite `ThemeProvider`, which applies it after first paint.
- Do write sentence case; don't use title case, all caps labels (the short page and section eyebrow is the one exception) or exclamation marks.
- Do use invented brands and reserved `.example` hosts in demo data (`reports.tollmere.example`); don't use `example.com` placeholders on stage or borrowed sample brands such as Northwind or Contoso.
- Don't leave shadcn defaults in the theme file (Geist, `--radius: 0.625rem`, the stock neutral values); run `scripts/check_theme.py` from this skill.
- Don't keep the stock component classes that break these rules after `shadcn add`. Seen in shadcn 4.22.1 `base-nova` components on 23 October 2026:
  - `dark:` colour overrides (button outline and ghost, badge, tabs, input and textarea `dark:bg-input/30`);
  - text dimmed with opacity: `TraceDescription` `/70`, `SidebarGroupLabel` `text-sidebar-foreground/70`, `TabsTrigger` `text-foreground/60`;
  - the `ring-ring/50` focus shadow in place of the 2 px outline (button, badge, tabs, trace, input, textarea);
  - the press `translate-y-px` and `hover:bg-primary/80` on the primary button (use `primary-hover` and `primary-active`);
  - `transition-all` (button, badge, tabs, `SidebarRail`) and `ease-linear` on the sidebar width (use named properties and `ease-out`);
  - the sheet scrim `bg-black/10` with `backdrop-blur-xs` (use the scrim above, no blur);
  - the `message-scroller` jump button's 400 ms exit (`data-[active=false]:duration-400` with a custom ease-in curve); cap it at 200 ms.

## Motion

Motion explains a change of state; it is never decoration.

- **Durations:** 150 ms for hover, press, menus, tooltips and a new turn's entrance; 200 ms for collapsibles, Trace rows, the folded-steps line, the sidebar collapse, the side panel, dialogs and sheets. Nothing longer.
- **Landing card lift:** the How it works and What you get feature cards rise 4 px on hover with a deeper light-mode shadow (`shadow-card-hover`), 200 ms `ease-out` on `translate` and `box-shadow` only; no lift with `prefers-reduced-motion`. Buttons never translate.
- **Easing:** enter with `cubic-bezier(0, 0, 0.2, 1)`, exit with `cubic-bezier(0.4, 0, 1, 1)` (Tailwind 4.3.3's `ease-out` and `ease-in`). No bounce, spring, parallax or looping motion other than the single streaming indicator.
- **Turn entrance:** a new turn fades in and rises 4 px over 150 ms (opacity and transform only). Streaming text, each token, trace-line text changes and anything the person triggered from the keyboard get no animation. Popovers and hover cards grow from their trigger using Base UI's `--transform-origin`, never from `scale(0)`.
- **Scrolling:** follow the stream only while the reader is at the bottom and release on any upward scroll. If shadcn's `message-scroller` (`@shadcn/react` 0.3.1) does this, cap its jump button's exit at 200 ms and scroll instantly under reduced motion.
- **Theme switch:** suppress transitions for one frame while the theme class changes.
- **Reduced motion:** under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`, remove translate and scale, keep only opacity changes of 100 ms or less, scroll instantly, and stop the shimmer (shadcn's `tailwind.css` already stops it; keep `motion-reduce:shimmer-none`).

## Brand override

A client brand may change only these tokens; everything else is fixed.

1. **Accent ramp:** `primary`, `primary-hover`, `primary-active`, `primary-foreground`, `accent`, `accent-foreground`, `ring`, `chart-1`, and their `dark-` twins (the `sidebar-primary*` and `sidebar-ring` tokens follow by reference). Keep the ramp's roles: primary carries white-ish text, accent is the subtle step, ring is focus only.
2. **Font families:** the `fontFamily` values and the matching `--font-sans` and `--font-mono` stacks, only if the client holds a web licence for self-hosting, keeping the three roles and the house fallbacks. Sizes, weights and line heights stay. The skill's `apply_brand.py` takes Fontsource packages; a licensed commercial font is self-hosted and mapped to the same three variables by hand, and the client's brand otherwise applies with the house fonts.
3. **Logo:** an SVG or image in the sidebar's workspace slot and as the favicon, with alternative text (empty when the workspace name sits next to it).
4. **Radius (optional):** one step for controls, 4 px or 8 px instead of 6 px. Nothing rounder. The 8 px card step and the 12 px step for the composer, side panel, sheets and dialogs do not change.

Neutrals, semantic colours, the `*-subtle` status surfaces, borders, spacing, density, elevation (including the composer shadow and the scroll fade), motion, focus treatment, the conversation patterns and copy rules do not change. After any override, `npx -y @google/design.md@0.4.0 lint` must report zero errors and zero warnings, and every 3:1 pair in the Colors table must be recomputed and still pass. If a pair fails, adjust the client step, never the floor.
